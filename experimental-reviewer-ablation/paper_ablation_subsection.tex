\subsubsection{\textbf{Ablation Study}}
\label{sec:ablation-reviewer}

To isolate the contribution of each pipeline stage, we conducted a controlled
component ablation under a \emph{common} configuration (identical fleet scenarios,
descriptor node sets, seeds, graph settings where applicable, clustering, campaign
gate, and metrics). Absolute metric values from this study are not claimed to be
identical to the frozen historical publication tables; the goal is relative
component attribution. We compare four variants: local Isolation Forest only (M1);
direct behavioural-descriptor clustering (M2); a standard 2-layer GCN on the same
similarity graph (M3); and the full GraphSAGE fleet-correlation stack (M4).

Across ten seeds, descriptor clustering already recovers strong coordinated campaigns
reliably (Campaign F1 $1.000\pm0.000$) but fails on weak campaigns
($0.000\pm0.000$). GraphSAGE improves weak-campaign detection
($0.500\pm0.527$) without increasing incorrect merging of unrelated incidents
(merge rate $0.000$), whereas the GCN baseline collapses campaign recovery
($0.000$) and merges unrelated attacks (merge rate $1.000$). Gains from GraphSAGE
over descriptor clustering are therefore scenario-dependent and clearest for weak
coordination; GraphSAGE does \emph{not} dominate descriptor clustering on strong
campaigns in this controlled setting.
